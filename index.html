v3.0 Free forever Runs on-device No tracking

01 Why NEXUS exists

I built NEXUS because I was tired of paying for AI tools just to try them. Free trials that expire. Subscriptions for tools I used twice. And the creeping feeling that the best AI experiences were locked behind a paywall.

So I built my own. No frameworks, no dependencies, no build step — just vanilla web technology and a whole lot of coffee. NEXUS runs entirely in your browser. It's fast, private, and free. And if you ever want more firepower, you bring your own API key — when you're ready, not when a countdown timer says you should be.

02 The honest feature list

Some features here will genuinely save you time. Others... exist because building them was fun. Both are yours to explore — no sales pitch attached.

Voice input NEW

Speak your question. Works offline with Web Speech API. Genuinely useful when your hands are busy.

Chat folders NEW

Organize conversations into colorful folders. Rename, move, delete. Your chats, your way.

Read aloud

Every AI message has a Read button. Close your eyes and listen. Works without a key.

Bring your own key

No key? Free local mode. Want GPT-4o? Paste your key. Always optional, never pushed.

File uploads

Attach images, code files, or text docs. Compressed locally before sending. Max 4 per message.

Edit & regenerate

Fix a typo in your message, resend, and watch the reply flow again. No re-typing required.

Keyboard shortcuts

Press ? to see them all. Some are hidden. Yes, that's on purpose.

Full theme system

Dark and light modes. System prompt presets. Temperature control. All the knobs, no gatekeeping.

05 Privacy, plain and simple

Your data stays yours

Chats, folders, files, and settings live only in your browser's localStorage. Nothing is uploaded unless you explicitly use the OpenAI API with your own key. No telemetry, no analytics, no tracking pixels. When you clear your browser data, NEXUS forgets everything. That's the whole design.
